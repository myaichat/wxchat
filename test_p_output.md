# What's New in Chelsea Art Space — Saturday, July 19, 2025

## Special Events and Closings Today

- **Steve McQueen at Dia Chelsea**
  - *Final day*: Last chance to experience Steve McQueen’s immersive, acclaimed exhibition at Dia Chelsea, 537 West 22nd St. The show explores space, sound, and moving image as interconnected acts of art[8].

- **Turned and Turning Still at The Painting Center**
  - *Closing reception*: 4–6 pm. A group exhibition curated by Kellie Lehr, Jamie Powell, and Lisa Petker Mintz, featuring works that embrace movement, change, and material experimentation. Main Gallery and Project Room, The Painting Center, 547 W 27th St[3].

- **Into the NOW — The Time of Our Lives at Ceres Gallery**
  - *Final day*: Exhibition celebrating the centennial of the New York Society of Women Artists. Features 49 women artists working in multiple media. Ceres Gallery, 547 West 27th St[7].

- **Summer City Idyll at Agora Gallery**
  - *Dates*: July 16–22, 2025. Vibrant annual group show by AGI Fine Art, featuring local and international contemporary artists. Agora Gallery, 530 West 25th St[5].

## Highlight Exhibitions

- **Salman Toor at Luhring Augustine Chelsea**
  - *Ongoing*: First solo show in Chelsea featuring new paintings exploring contemporary identity, private and public moments. Paintings are displayed in Chelsea, with works on paper in Tribeca[1][2].

- **Major Postwar French Avant-Garde**
  - Significant group exhibition with more than forty postwar works curated by Beverley Calté and Arnauld Pierre, exploring a unique period of French avant-garde art[1][2].

- **A Love Letter to New York**
  - Group show dedicated to the city’s spirit, featuring works by John Baeder, Danielle DeJesus, David DeSimone, Richard Haas, and others[2].

- **Notable Media and Sculpture**
  - Exhibits feature figurative sculpture in wood and bronze, glass-layered works, and new prints by William Kentridge across Chelsea and Tribeca spaces[1][2].

## Gallery Visit Information

- Most Chelsea galleries are **open Tuesday–Saturday, 10 am–6 pm**. Some are not open today (Saturday), so it's recommended to check with individual galleries for holiday or weekend schedules[1].
- Final days for several landmark summer exhibitions — consider revisiting or catching receptions if in the neighborhood.
- The area is walkable, making it easy to tour multiple galleries and catch seasonal shows and closings.
